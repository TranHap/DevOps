## 1. Setup and fairness protocol

| model | ranked | learning regime | labels used | training sessions | score | decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RF + MCV | yes | supervised | full | 402542 | predict_proba[:, 1] | score >= 0.5 |
| LSTM + BERT | yes | supervised | full | 402542 | max window P(anomaly) (Part 1 merging) | score >= 0.5 |
| PCA | yes | unsupervised | none | 402542 | SPE (squared prediction error) | score > Q-statistic |
| LogGPT | yes | semi-supervised | normal-only filter | 5000 | max rank of the actual next key | score > K |
| LogGPT w/o RL | no (ablation) | semi-supervised | normal-only filter | 5000 | max rank of the actual next key | score > K |
| PCA (5,000 normal) | no (ablation) | semi-supervised (normal-only) | normal-only filter | 5000 | SPE (squared prediction error) | score > Q-statistic |

Training data per resample: RF and LSTM use the whole train split with labels (the LSTM holds 10% of it out for epoch selection). PCA uses the whole train split with its labels replaced by −1. LogGPT uses 5,000 normal sessions sampled from the train split (4,500 for training, 500 for validation); labels are used only for this filter. No model uses test data or anomaly labels for any training-time decision.

## 8. Threats to validity

- **Different label usage.** RF and LSTM learn from all labels, LogGPT only from the knowledge that its training sessions are normal, PCA from no label at all. The ranking compares learning regimes, not only algorithms.
- **Different training-set sizes.** LogGPT trains on 5,000 sessions (paper protocol), the supervised models on 402,542 sessions.
- **Temporal leakage.** The random session-level split places later blocks in the training set (Part 1, D-10).
- **Fixed thresholds.** K (50% of the training keys) and PCA's Q-statistic threshold come from the original papers/code and were not tuned; the AUC column shows the threshold-free view. K itself moves with the sample: the 5,000 training sessions contain 15–17 unique keys, so K is 7 or 8 depending on the seed (§5, post-hoc sensitivity).
- **One dataset.** HDFS only (BGL windows are ≈ 50% anomalous, which breaks the rare-anomaly assumption of the unsupervised detectors).
- **Implementation.** LogGPT was ported to current libraries and fed Part 1's sessions; its scoring was verified against the original code (§4), but GPU kernels are not bit-reproducible.
